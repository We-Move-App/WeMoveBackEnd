When you create or use your account, we may collect:

WeMove All may automatically collect technical information about your device such as:

With your permission, WeMove All may collect location information including:

5. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

WeMove All shares only the minimum information required to provide services through the platform.

A. Transportation Bookings (Bike, Taxi)

When you request a ride, the following information may be shared with the driver:

  • your name
  • phone number
  • pickup location
  • drop-off location
  • live location for navigation

Drivers do not receive your ID documents, email address, or payment details.

This information is necessary to complete the ride safely.

B. Bus Bookings

When you book a bus ticket, the following information may be shared with the transport provider:

  • passenger name
  • phone number (sometimes)
  • ticket number
  • QR code
  • seat number
  • travel route

This information is required to confirm your reservation and verify boarding.

C. Hotel Bookings

When you reserve a hotel through the platform, the following information may be shared with the hotel:

  • your name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • booking reference
  • check-in and check-out dates
  • room type
  • number of guests

Hotels do not receive your ID document unless required by law during check-in.

D. Payment Processing

WeMove All may share necessary payment information with:

  • mobile money providers
  • banks
  • payment processors

Shared information may include:

  • name
  • wallet ID
  • transaction ID
  • payment amount
  • required verification data

This information is necessary to process transactions securely.

E. Third-Party Technical Services

WeMove All may share limited technical information with trusted third-party service providers responsible for:

  • cloud hosting
  • analytics services
  • crash monitoring systems

This may include:

  • crash reports
  • performance data
  • device information

WeMove All does not sell personal information for advertising.

F. Legal Requests

WeMove All may disclose personal information when legally required by:

  • law enforcement authorities
  • courts
  • government agencies

Only the information requested through lawful procedures will be shared.
