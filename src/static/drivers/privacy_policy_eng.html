1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WeMove All collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of drivers operating on the WeMove All platform, including both bike and taxi drivers.

By registering and using the WeMove All platform as a driver, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the platform.

2. Information We Collect

WeMove All collects the following types of information from drivers:

A. Personal Information

· Full name

· Phone number and email address

· National ID, passport, or driver’s license

· Profile photo

B. Vehicle & Driving Information

· Vehicle type, registration number, insurance details

· Driver’s license validity

· Vehicle condition and inspection reports

C. Financial & Transaction Information

· Bank or mobile wallet account details

· Payment history, earnings, and transaction IDs

· Withdrawal records

D. Location & Usage Data

· GPS location during rides

· Ride logs, routes, and trip history

· App usage data, crash logs, and analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

Driver information is used to:

· Verify identity and driver eligibility

· Assign rides and calculate earnings

· Process payments, withdrawals, and financial reports

· Monitor safety, compliance, and fraud prevention

· Respond to support inquiries and complaints

· Improve the platform’s performance and services

· Comply with legal obligations and law enforcement requests

4. Sharing Your Information

WeMove All shares driver information only when necessary, including:

· With passengers: Name, vehicle type, and live location during rides

· With authorities: In case of accidents, disputes, fraud, or legal requests

· With service partners: Payment processors, insurance companies, or inspection agencies

· Internally: Analytics, crash monitoring, and security audits

Important: WeMove All will never sell or misuse your personal data for advertising.

5. Fraud, Investigations, and Suspensions

· If fraud or suspicious activity is reported, WeMove All may temporarily suspend the driver and passenger accounts while investigating.

· Drivers must provide screenshots, trip logs, or any other relevant evidence if requested.

· WeMove All may share information with police or courts to resolve disputes.

· Drivers must never share OTP, passwords, or PINs. Any misuse of personal credentials is the driver’s responsibility.

6. Data Security

WeMove All uses industry-standard measures to protect driver data:

· Encrypted storage for sensitive information

· HTTPS/SSL encryption for data transmission

· Restricted internal access to personal information

· Regular security audits and backups

· Drivers may request deletion of their account and personal data, except for financial records required by law.
