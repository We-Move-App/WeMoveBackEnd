2. Eligibility & Driver Requirements

Drivers must be 18 years or older.

Must possess a valid motorcycle or taxi driver’s license recognized in Cameroon.

Must provide government-issued identification.

Must have a valid insurance policy covering passengers, third parties, and personal liability.

3. Driver Account Creation & Verification

Provide accurate personal information: full name, phone number, email, identification documents.

Only verified drivers can accept ride assignments.

Accounts may be suspended or terminated if verification fails or fraudulent activity is detected.

Drivers are responsible for securing their login credentials.

4. Driver Obligations & Responsibilities

Accept ride assignments promptly and maintain professional conduct.

Verify passenger details and ensure safe pickup and drop-off.

Follow traffic laws, regulations, and WeMove All guidelines.

Maintain your vehicle in safe, operational condition.

Keep documentation ready for verification: registration, license, and insurance.

Example: Drivers must not accept rides while impaired, overloading passengers, or bypassing safety standards.

5. Vehicle Requirements & Safety Standards

Vehicle must be in good working condition: brakes, lights, tires, mirrors, seat belts (taxi).

Helmets are mandatory for bike riders, and taxi drivers must follow safety regulations.

Regular maintenance and servicing are the driver’s responsibility.

WeMove All reserves the right to request proof of vehicle maintenance or inspection.

6. Ride Assignment & Operations

Rides are assigned through the WeMove All app based on proximity, availability, and driver performance rating.

Drivers may accept or decline rides; repeated refusals may affect account status.

GPS tracking is enabled for passenger safety, monitoring, and operational efficiency.

Drivers are responsible for accurate navigation and safe transportation.

7. Payments, Wallet Transactions & Daily Limits

Drivers are paid through the WeMove All wallet after ride completion.

Daily transaction limit: 500,000 CFA (may increase at platform discretion without prior notice).

Transaction Reversals: WeMove All does not guarantee reversals.

Before confirming a transfer, drivers and passengers must verify beneficiary details carefully.

Reversal requests due to disputes between users may result in temporary account suspension until investigation is complete.

Fraud detection: report suspected fraud immediately with screenshots and evidence; WeMove All will investigate and take action promptly.

Drivers may not solicit payments outside the platform.
